Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

This policy explains what information Orbit Print Studio ("we", "us") collects and how we use it. It covers this website, our Etsy shops (Orbit Print Studio and ClearPath Budget Shop), and Orbit Studio Publisher, the private tool we use to manage our own shops and post to our own social media accounts.

Who we are

Orbit Print Studio is a small independent design studio selling original products on Etsy. Contact: orbitprintstudio@gmail.com.

This website

This website does not use accounts, forms, cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers. Our host (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

When you buy from our Etsy shops

Purchases happen on Etsy, under Etsy's Privacy Policy. To fill your order we receive what Etsy shares with sellers, such as your name, shipping address, and order details. We use it only to make, ship, and support your order. Physical items are printed and shipped by our production partner (Printify and its print providers), which receives the details needed to deliver your order. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for marketing unless you contact us.

If you message us, we keep the conversation so we can help you. Our tools may draft replies for us to review; a person reviews and sends every reply.

Orbit Studio Publisher (our own tool)

Orbit Studio Publisher is used only by Orbit Print Studio to manage accounts we own. It is not offered to other people or businesses. With our permission, given through each platform's official sign-in (OAuth), it connects to:

  • Instagram: to publish photos and carousels to our own account and read basic profile information and post results.
  • TikTok: to publish posts to our own account and read basic profile information.
  • Pinterest: to create pins on our own boards and read our boards and pin results.
  • Etsy: to read and update our own shop's information and listings (titles, tags, descriptions, and the shop announcement). It does not request access to buyers' personal information.

What it stores: the access tokens each platform issues, our own posts and their basic results (such as views), and our own shop and listing data. This is kept on a private server we control, which is not reachable from the public internet, except for a public folder containing only finished post images that the platforms fetch when we publish.

What it does not do: it does not collect information about other users, does not read anyone's private messages, does not sell or share data with third parties, and does not use platform data to train AI models. Text for our posts and listings may be drafted with AI tools from our own product information.

How long we keep it: tokens are kept until we disconnect the account or the platform expires them. Post and shop records are kept while the business operates, and deleted on request.

Data deletion

To delete data Orbit Studio Publisher holds about an account, email orbitprintstudio@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and the account name. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. You can also remove the app's access at any time in the platform's settings (for example, Instagram: Settings, then Apps and websites).

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to how we use it. Email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Our shops and tools are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Security

We use access controls, encrypted connections, and a private network for our tools. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify affected people and platforms promptly if a breach occurs.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date above.
